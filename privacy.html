Habitat TrailsHelp & support

Privacy & your data

Updated 19 October 2026 · Production version 1.0 (build 8)

The production app does not collect or transmit personal data to the developer or third-party advertising or analytics services. It requires no player account and includes no advertising SDK, live ads or in-app purchases. Race content, progress and controls work locally on your device. Coins and cars are earned through play.

Earlier test build

TestFlight build 7 is different: it contains opt-in sample-ad testing in Garage using Google Mobile Ads. Enabling it connects to Google, whose SDK may process IP addresses and inferred approximate location, device or app identifiers, advertising interactions, crash and performance information for advertising and analytics. No advertising identifier permission is requested. See Google privacy information. Those sample-ad features and SDK are not included in production build 8.

What is saved locally

Your settings, selected course and car, coins, unlocked items, race and lap records, and personal-best ghost are stored on your device. There is no game-operated cloud sync or online leaderboard. If storage is unavailable, some progress lasts only for the current session.

Optional controls

When you enable tilt steering, the game uses device motion to steer. These readings are processed during play, are not saved as a sensor history, and are not transmitted. You can switch back to buttons or a steering wheel in Controls & settings. Sound and haptics use your chosen preferences. The game does not request microphone, camera, contacts or location access.

Diagnostics and support

Earlier test builds store a local startup check to help diagnose loading problems. It can include the local page address, displayed status and readiness. A single bounded performance summary is also saved locally when you pause or finish: course, car, graphics tier, frame intervals and up to eight stutter locations within the game. It contains no lap times or personal-best records. Neither diagnostic is uploaded automatically. The support page lets you view and copy a small technical summary; nothing is sent unless you choose to share it yourself. Do not include personal information in screenshots or reports.

Network and backups

The installed game loads its bundled files through a server on the device itself. It does not download race content from an external service. Operating-system backups or diagnostics may be handled by Apple or your device provider under their own settings and policies. If you play the web version on a hosted website, that host may process connection information; this notice describes the bundled offline app.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes its local game data. Offloading the app may keep that data. Device backups may retain copies or restore progress later; manage those separately in your device settings. We do not hold a server copy of your game progress.

Support messages and your choices

If you contact support, your email address, message and attachments are used to investigate and respond. Contact the support address below to request access, correction or deletion of messages you sent. Do not send sensitive information. Your email provider and our support email provider handle messages under their own privacy policies. The Google policy above applies only to the earlier sample-ad test build. There are no player accounts to delete in this version.

Children and future changes

The game is being prepared for a broad audience. Live advertising is not enabled in this build. Age-appropriate ad treatment and applicable consent choices must be implemented before live ads are offered. Parents or guardians may contact support about information sent by a child.
