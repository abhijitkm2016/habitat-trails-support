Privacy & your data

Privacy notice · Updated 19 October 2026 · Current playtest with optional sample ads

Habitat Trails does not require an account. This version does not send your gameplay, personal information or device sensor readings to us. Live advertising and purchases are disabled. The iPhone build includes Google Mobile Ads SDK for optional sample-ad testing. If you enable sample ads in Garage, it connects to Google to load test advertising; Google’s SDK may process IP addresses (including an approximate location inferred from them), device or app identifiers, advertising interactions, crash information and performance information for advertising and analytics. These practices apply to the third-party SDK; local race records and motion readings are not deliberately shared with it by the game. No advertising identifier permission is requested. Racing content remains bundled and works without ads. See Google privacy information. Production consent and store privacy disclosures must be completed before live advertising.

What is saved locally

Your settings, selected course and car, coins, unlocked items, race and lap records, and personal-best ghost are stored on your device. There is no game-operated cloud sync or online leaderboard. If storage is unavailable, some progress lasts only for the current session.

Optional controls

When you enable tilt steering, the game uses device motion to steer. These readings are processed during play, are not saved as a sensor history, and are not transmitted. You can switch back to buttons or a steering wheel in Controls & settings. Sound and haptics use your chosen preferences. The game does not request microphone, camera, contacts or location access.

Diagnostics and support

The iPhone playtest stores a local startup check to help diagnose loading problems. It can include the local page address, displayed status and readiness. A single bounded performance summary is also saved locally when you pause or finish: course, car, graphics tier, frame intervals and up to eight stutter locations within the game. It contains no lap times or personal-best records. Neither diagnostic is uploaded automatically. The support page lets you view and copy a small technical summary; nothing is sent unless you choose to share it yourself. Do not include personal information in screenshots or reports.

Network and backups

The installed game loads its bundled files through a server on the device itself. It does not download race content from an external service. Operating-system backups or diagnostics may be handled by Apple or your device provider under their own settings and policies. If you play the web version on a hosted website, that host may process connection information; this notice describes the bundled offline app.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes its local game data. Offloading the app may keep that data. Device backups may retain copies or restore progress later; manage those separately in your device settings. We do not hold a server copy of your game progress.

Support messages and your choices

If you contact support, your email address, message and attachments are used to investigate and respond. Contact the support address below to request access, correction or deletion of messages you sent. Do not send sensitive information. Third-party services manage their own retention and privacy choices; see Google’s linked policy for its practices. There are no player accounts to delete in this version.

Children and future changes

The game is being prepared for a broad audience. Live advertising is not enabled in this build. Age-appropriate ad treatment and applicable consent choices must be implemented before live ads are offered. Parents or guardians may contact support about information sent by a child.
