Include your device, course, car, control method and what happened. A screenshot helps. Do not send passwords, payment details or sensitive information.

Steering and tilt

Choose your controls in Settings. For tilt, hold the phone comfortably and still while it calibrates. Recalibrate after changing orientation or grip. Buttons remain available as a fallback.

Progress and purchases

Current test-build progress is saved on your device. Deleting the app can remove it. Do not delete the app to troubleshoot. Live purchases are not available in the current Release test build.

Sound and performance

Sound and music have separate volume controls in Settings. If the game stutters, note the course location and whether you recently resumed the app or watched a test ad. You can copy a local technical summary from the in-app support page and choose to email it.

Privacy requests

Use the same support email for requests about information you have sent us. No message is sent automatically by this page.
